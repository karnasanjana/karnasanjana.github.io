Research · PyTorch · medical imaging

KnoBo reproduction

Problem
Medical image models break when they meet a new hospital's data.
Built
Reproduced a NeurIPS 2024 concept-bottleneck method, then fixed a bug that silently corrupted ablations, made training ~6× faster, and replaced test-set model selection with a proper held-out split.
Result
Confirmed +8.8 pts out-of-domain over baselines, and showed the original protocol overstated accuracy by ~1.8 pts.

NLP · deep learning · evaluation

Fake News Detector

Problem
A 99% score looked too good: 99.8% of real articles in the data mention "Reuters".
Built
Stripped every source giveaway, compared against TF-IDF baselines, and trained a 1D-CNN with the tokenizer saved inside the model.
Result
99.3% on 5,864 held-out articles, now a number that survives scrutiny. Served with Streamlit.
